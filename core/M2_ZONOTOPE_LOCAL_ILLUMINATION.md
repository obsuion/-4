## Accounting (local resource policy 1)

Scalar leaves and v3 reservations are unchanged and reported separately. New
controls are finite precharged schedules, not measured operation counts or time:
`SET_SCAN = 1024 + 90*(64+4096)` covers full six-center/twelve-generator signatures
and rank-bounded Decimal tuple scans. Per new step reserve four such scans plus
B33 cell-copy control plus 4096; guarded numerical work is additional. Each pass
prepays complete ledger/final audits, piece controls, exact planning, allocations
and terminal retention. Every seed's bounded ancestry scan is separately charged.
Input metadata scan/hash is prepaid from bounded structure/text lengths.

Geometry leaf rational operations are cumulative, with the accepted 512-per-cell
and 16384-bit guards. Separate 4096-unit controls cover conversion, source/Sun
binding, retention and each conservative closed-overlay pair allowance. Combined
geometry total is leaves plus controls, bounded by the explicit policy. Controls
never represent newly executed ray leaves. Scalar total is likewise leaves plus
reservations, capped by the accepted 2**31 ceiling; budgets never reset per trial.

Prospective step, trial, pass, worst-case Picard, geometry and evidence counts are
admitted before transport. Exact planning/control and geometry 512-cell work are
reserved prospectively. No fabricated full v3 leaf upper bound is claimed: guarded
actual scalar exhaustion during a kernel is atomic computational failure.

Retained evidence units are fixed-schema slots, not bytes: root 96; each planned
step 96 (full set, lineage/signatures/tube); each geometry cell 64 (certificate,
support/signature and publication references); each trial 8; each pass 16.
Initial and final views share immutable records. Maximum 32768 global steps keeps
all 32*step+axis generator births strictly within the existing 2**20 domain.
Every trial has at most the unchanged base max_steps <=4096. Passes <=16, trials
<=64, geometry <=65536, evidence <=1048576; no unbounded retries or hidden method.

Validation/results and exact reproduction commands are in the bound external
review evidence. No registries, dispatch, executors, checkpoints or accepted
source files change. General speedup and full-H5 improvement are not established.

## Bounded validation contract

The selected final gate covers the two new test modules and fifteen accepted
zonotope/segment/geometry/window/local modules. Initial scientific partitions are
compared in full against B33/B34, including exact Decimal tuples, Fraction supports,
whole tubes, affine/RN Sun bounds, ray coefficients and classifications. Independent
short-circle whole-arc series bounds enclose initial/refined/approach tubes.
Synthetic tangent contact is checked exactly. Quota and tamper tests are failures,
not physical evidence. Final counts and all earlier failures are bound externally.

Two frozen short offline workers publish complete typed scientific outputs. The
fresh replay must match each primary byte, including computational/budget failures.
The primary also replays exactly one fixed B3, B4, B6 and B33 archived control; full
published legacy scientific data/counters are compared, with no timing fields.
Public B33/B34 results are references only and cannot seed the new query. No long
H5, numerical speed study, dispatch integration or external authority is involved.

`initial_attempted_steps`/`refinement_attempted_steps` count new owner's transport
entries; inherited `attempted_steps` is the frozen kernel counter (a very early
kernel resource failure may occur before its counter increments). Geometry attempt
and successful-record counters are also separate. Failure retention remains inside
the prospectively reserved fixed-schema evidence units. Full-set signature/rank
control schedules are deliberately conservative; the static six-file candidate
makes no scalability or full-H5 resource-admission claim.
