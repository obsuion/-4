# YEAR-V5 review candidate

This candidate connects an explicitly versioned quiet rejection method and an
explicitly versioned packed terminal storage policy to the existing serial year
executor. It is derived from the accepted YEAR-V4 SOURCE04. Historical methods,
stores and source identities remain distinct and are not migrated in place.

`year_project.compile_job_v5` preserves the authored project, derives only the
declared duration and quiet method, records that derivation, and requires the
packed storage policy. `admit_job` independently recomputes and validates the
derivation before creating the ordinary lifecycle root/store. It does not change
physical coefficients, numerical tolerances, event boundaries or selected leaf
arithmetic. The old `compile_job` entry remains available for its own legacy
formats and policy.

The new quiet work record charges performed calls and records skipped calls
separately. A rejected full/first trial may avoid unneeded later trials; it never
selects a full trial instead of the established fine or detailed path. Unexpected
errors are not known physical refusals. A skipped error norm is not measured.
See YEAR_V5_QUIET.md for the exact method and guard boundaries.

The packed status adapter retains lossless canonical scientific streams. New
bounded block/index and terminal namespace ownership are separate from the old
per-file pool. Integrity is checked at seal, consume, restore/export and explicit
verified admission. Ordinary progress does not promise a global historical-byte
rehash at every poll. See YEAR_V5_STORAGE.md for limits and trust boundaries.

Cross-version acceptance uses a prospectively frozen physical projection:
physical state, selected events/actions/refusals, product credit, clocks and
physical reports remain exact; method/source identities and genuine numerical
work may differ and remain available in the raw views. Same-version fresh
continuation requires the full corresponding scientific/work views to match,
with restore overhead reported separately.

All actual gates, timing qualification, failures, cumulative expenses and
unfinished obligations are recorded in the external YEAR-V5 review evidence.
This document is a contract, not a claim that a gate or year calculation passed.
The year job is prepared DATA only. A successful day and synthetic storage corpus
do not by themselves establish annual storage capacity or physical validation.
