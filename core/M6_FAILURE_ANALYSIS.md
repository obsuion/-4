# B48 first bounded M6 diagnosis and controlled engineering intervention

`M6_TYPED_RETURNED_EVIDENCE_DIAGNOSIS_V1` consumes actual typed coordinator
history outside selection. Each immutable finding binds root/model/assembly/
construction/controller/estimation identities, decision/action/request IDs,
time, exact returned owner reason and field-level evidence with native units.
Forecast trial, committed actual action, received ack, policy screen, model
estimate and evaluation-only actual receipt/credit are separate scopes.

Sampled visibility and FOV are distinct from declared station availability, RF
admission and nominal service rate. Returned camera separation is a derivation
from the returned pose/directions and declared camera axis, not a new geometry
certificate. Forecast thermal diagnostics remain private trial endpoints;
they are not actual observed spacecraft temperatures. An acquisition/storage
combined refusal or service/geometry combined reason retains UNKNOWN subcause.
Unsupported labels do not become inferred battery, thermal or flight failures.
An unselected request is a policy outcome; greedy lower candidates can remain
untried. No global optimum or hardware winner is asserted.

`M6_TWO_ROOT_CONTROLLED_INTERVENTION_V1` admits exactly two immutable roots and
finite aggregate decision/forecast/output bounds: at most 64 total decisions and
512 forecasts. Before each boundary the owner reserves a complete max-candidate
allowance. Separate case physics ledgers remain owned; scientific hashing is
paid reencoding in each case and comparison publication is paid in the baseline
ledger. No spent work is reset by the experiment owner.

Estimation comparison binds an
exact received-only reference to the opt-in command root. A payload-wire-area
intervention permits only `payload-line.wire.cross_section_m2` to change, then
requires new exact construction and route-mass bindings. All world, requests,
priorities, latency, controller, FOV, thermal/protection, routing positions and
other wire properties remain common. Harness mass, COM and all tensor entries
are recompiled independently; hardware is never overwritten mid-flight.

`examples.m6_engineering_comparison` runs the production comparison owner and
collector with `--hypothesis wire` or `placement`. It records both actual
histories and output differences. Synthetic wire areas 1e-10 and 1e-6 m^2 are
ASSUMED stress/repair inputs using existing copper-like material/current/drop
assumptions; they establish no flight design recommendation. The experiment
reports delivered output and resources together. A controller that avoids a
forecast electrical failure has not observed that failure in actual execution.
More battery because no product was acquired is not better mission performance.

No general optimizer, random campaign, automatic topology rewrite, RF model,
interval event accuracy, physical validation or complete M6 workflow is provided.
Exact measured comparisons and source identities are in the external review
evidence. Full Version M and wider continuous/FDIR/recovery work remain open.

Typed termination evidence distinguishes admission/output exhaustion,
computational exceptions, incomplete coordinator prefixes and owned process
timeouts. None establishes future coverage, an endpoint or physical failure.
The CLI retains the original exception/traceback and secondary capture errors
without suppressing the original. Deeper causes remain UNKNOWN.
