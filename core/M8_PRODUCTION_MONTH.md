# B54 production lifecycle contract

This candidate repairs production integration and static admission. The code
below does not establish a completed day/month, clean final regression,
Windows/HTTP/DOM acceptance, physical qualification, or Version M completion.
Those claims require separately bound actual evidence. Historical B53 failures
and preservation findings remain unchanged.

## Progress and legacy boundaries

The world owner retains native identity/snapshot API
`B52_NOMINAL_LIFECYCLE_WORLD_V1`. Its observational accounting now explicitly
uses `B54_LIFECYCLE_WORLD_ACCOUNTING_V2`, including the declared
`ONLY_EXPLICIT_SELECTED_SUPPORT_COMMITS` tracking policy. Current detailed and
quiet producers pass the same strict plain/chunked ProgressReader validator.
Unknown/missing fields are rejected. The frozen old exact accounting shape is
accepted only for the legacy lifecycle method; ambiguous old-version extended
shapes and downgrades of new-method accounting are rejected.

Selected illumination tallies use seconds and exact canonical rational pairs.
Their nonnegative illuminated/occulted/unresolved sum equals consumed support.
New-method ACTUAL support ends at the committed physical clock. FORECAST can
inherit only an ACTUAL prefix; its queries cannot claim future consumed time.
Legacy detailed roots explicitly retain an empty tracked tally rather than
inventing full illumination coverage. This is sampled/held nominal geometry,
not continuous eclipse or event-time certification.

Quiet accounting retains every actual QuietWork field, including finite
`maximum_accepted_error_norm` in [0,1], current controller clock and exact work
balances. A rejected trial's error is not a maximum accepted error or actual
temperature. Detailed roots legitimately have no quiet controller/work.

## Compiled static admission

METHOD53 production uses `B54_FROZEN_INPUT_ADMISSION_V1`; the legacy method
continues baseline admission. This source-pinned choice is structural, not a
new integration schedule or numerical approximation. Original immutable inputs
receive full canonical constructor admission. Process-local seals then check
frozen owner/field identities and mutable Fraction/Enum representations.
Mutable physical states, contexts, trial results, requests, counters and caches
are not reused as static truth. Schema-5 dynamic capture/reconstruction,
losslessness checks, restore, limits, protection, role/root/source/runtime and
current-state validation remain enforced. Foreign checkpoint/input documents
must pass the complete native trust boundary; no serialized cache is trusted.

Native compiled checkpoints use version 3 and persist separately accounted
ACTUAL/FORECAST wrapper counters. Scientific RuntimeWork and projections are
unchanged by these observational counters. A short exact comparison is bounded
evidence for its actual source/input; it is not a general speedup claim.

## Exact paid publication and continuation

`B54_CHECKPOINT_PUBLICATION_PREFIX_V2` binds native checkpoint bytes, scientific
stream prefixes and cumulative wrapper costs at the explicit
`BEFORE_CHECKPOINT_PROGRESS_PUBLICATION` boundary. The checkpoint progress
record and later terminal/failure/stream-finalization reservations occur after
that boundary. Four fixed per-stream reservation totals derive these deltas
exactly; loose greater-than-or-equal comparison is insufficient.

After all journals close, immutable `application-work-final.json` binds the
terminal/failure record hash and the whole settled attempt's publication costs.
Its own bounded metadata reservation is prepaid. Native stream manifests and
their final self-publication reservation reconcile exactly. Missing, partial,
failed-closure, inconsistent or foreign receipts do not grant MAIN trust.
Exports include these receipts and all selected ancestor artifacts by manifest.

Resume selects a native state at its original committed clock and inherits the
whole settled parent's publication costs separately. Static wrapper counters
also retain later parent costs: after native restore, the exact observational
merge is `whole_parent + (restored - selected_native_prior)` for every counter
in each role. Both roles and their finite ceilings are checked before either
ledger changes. Fresh restore work is counted once; scientific state,
RuntimeWork and controller knowledge are not replaced by later parent truth.
Subsequent native checkpoints persist the merged counters. Invalid merge or
failed observational capture is reported and cannot become a validated paid
prefix. Primary exceptions and secondary observation errors are retained
separately. No counter refund, clock retag, prefix replay or automatic retry is
authorized by this contract.

## Timing and evidence limits

`B54_DISJOINT_PUBLIC_CALL_TIMING_V1` records current-worker start/restore/step
attempts, normal returns, exceptions, wall nanoseconds and actual worker CPU
nanoseconds. These sequential scopes are observational, outside scientific
projections and restart authority. The remaining measured wall includes
wrapper, I/O, control and unclassified work; it is not a pure overhead estimate
or numerical-operation CPU accounting. Parent/private-Job CPU is separate.

All physical models, common-study assumptions, numerical resolutions and
selected/trial distinctions remain those of the authored root. A prospective
month, MAIN/loupe equality, resource growth, Stop/reopen/export and browser
behavior must be evaluated on their actual frozen source/input. No result or
completion claim is supplied by this contract document alone.
## Conditional finite segmented store policy

B54 also supplies the explicitly selected wrapper contract
`B54_FINITE_SEGMENTED_MAIN_LINEAGE_V1`. It is selected only at CREATE:
`LifecycleStore(path, root, create=True, runner_policy=LifecycleRunnerStorePolicy())`.
Its strict store format is `B54_FINITE_SEGMENTED_LIFECYCLE_STORE_V2`, with a
canonical SHA-256 policy identity, exactly eight jobs, exactly eight lineage
members, and a cumulative reservation of 1536 MiB. This is finite wrapper
capacity, not additional scientific work allowance or a claim of month success.

Omitting the selection preserves the V1 store, four-job population, and original
wire fields. Existing stores are never migrated. Reopening admits the stored
format, exact policy values, root/input/source/runtime identities, and rejects
mixed or unknown policy fields. A duplicate workspace root requested under a
different wrapper policy rejects rather than silently substituting its store.

Every prior job counts, including failed/cancelled jobs. Output reservations
are the sum of immutable intents, without refunds. The separate workspace
2 GiB ceiling, per-child bounds, source/task budgets, ownership rules, MAIN
paid-prefix rules, and export compressed/raw/member caps remain unchanged.
Eight slots do not promise eight 256 MiB jobs within the 1536 MiB reservation.
Lineage export uses the pinned limit and includes the policy-bearing store
metadata in its manifest. No automatic segmentation or retry is introduced.

CLI `create`/`configure` use explicit `--runner-store-policy` selection. HTTP
creation optionally accepts the same exact method string in
`runner_store_policy`, with `LEGACY` default. Both old request shapes remain
strict; the policy key may accompany either known shape. The UI shows the
selection and remaining slots/reservations, and blocks its default-size action
when the finite reservation is insufficient. The model/root numerical identity
does not include this external store policy; its immutable store metadata does.

The focused store-policy tests were written after the task test budget was
exhausted and were not run. Historical passing tests are not evidence for these
new cases. Actual production metadata/Stop/reopen/resume evidence, where
obtained, must be reported separately from unit-test qualification.
